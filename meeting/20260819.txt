[Q & A]
Q: Before and after surgery image comparison — showing texture or density?

A: Density only, not texture. (I'll add this feature in a future update)
The software includes a Contrast Mode that overlays a per-region density-change grid calculated as post minus pre. Blue indicates decreased density, and red indicates increased density.
Texture metrics (GLCM, Fractal Dimension, and BHI) are not included in this comparison.

Q: Do you show this patient over time?
A: Yes.
Each patient page opens with a BMD trend chart, showing the patient's density values across all available studies. The percentage change from the previous exam is also displayed.

Q: Can you show 100 patients?
A: Yes.
The patient list is a searchable, scrollable list (by name or MRN) with no fixed limit. However, the current interface is designed for reviewing one patient at a time rather than comparing multiple patients simultaneously.

Q: Could you combine data from many patients?
A: Not currently.(I'll add this feature in a future update)
The software does not currently support combining or analyzing multiple patients together, but this feature could be added if needed 

Q: What if there's a foreign body, such as cement, a bullet, or surgical hardware?
A: I need to correct my earlier statement.
I did build a metal detection algorithm, but I couldn't ship it because its reliability was too low to trust.
So for now, metal contamination must be verified visually, and any density value in those regions should be treated as unreliable.
I went back and reviewed the source code and my records, and I also noted this in my July 23rd report email.
