‘This is not replacing DXA. 
It extracts consistent and automated bone structures from images you already have. 
It extracts two things: density (how bright and dense the bones appear) and texture (the patterns within the bones).

However, this is only an approximation and is not corrected to actual bone density units.
===========
The pipeline consists of four stages.
It takes an X-ray image, has the AI ​​locate the L4 vertebra and outline it,
measures the L4 Bone structure in that area, and reports the values ​​along with a heatmap showing where the AI ​​found it.
I upgraded the model from Yolo 8 to Yolo 26 last week.
===========
I will show you the overall scope and specific methodology of this research.
If you look at Imaging modalities, there are various options ranging from standard diagnostic equipment like DXA to CT and MRI. Among these, what we focused on is X-ray.
The purpose of this project is to establish an 'Opportunistic screening' pipeline for osteoporosis risk.
===========
Our AI architecture is a pipeline that fuses Statistical Texture Analysis (GLCM), Deep Learning-based Segmentation (YOLO), and Explainable AI (Seg-Grad-CAM).
===========
For the training and validation datasets, 4,000 samples were used from the AP (Anteroposterior) and LA (Lateral) BUU datasets, and 42 DICOM files were used for the test dataset.

"Next, Our Bone Density and Structure Measurement Procedure.
This pipeline is currently under active research and is subject to continuous changes and updates

First, Region of Interest (ROI) Isolation: To focus purely on the microarchitecture that reflects bone loss, I remove the cortical bone and the top and bottom endplates. 
This isolation process allows us to extract and analyze the cancellous bone exclusively.

Second, Feature Extraction: From this isolated cancellous region, I compute the Bone Attenuation Ratio to capture overall density. Concurrently, to deeply analyze the bone’s structural patterns, i extract three categories of texture features: the GLCM5 metrics for local homogeneity and complexity, the Variation Slope, and the Fractal Dimension for structural self-similarity.

Future Scope: Currently, this pipeline is optimized to calculate the density and texture of the L4 vertebra specifically. 
As a next step to enhance diagnostic robustment, I plan to expand this scope to calculate and utilize the average bone mineral density across the L1 to L4 vertebrae.
